Scene Draw-Call Analyzer

Purpose: Analyze the rendering workload of a scene and identify what is responsible for individual draw calls.

Scope

This should be broader than the Canvas Batch Analyzer.

Analyze:

* SpriteRenderers
* MeshRenderers
* SkinnedMeshRenderers
* ParticleSystems
* UI
* Materials
* Shaders
* Textures
* Cameras
* Rendering layers
* Batching
* Instancing
