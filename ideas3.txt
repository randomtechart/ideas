Scene Draw-Call Analyzer

Purpose: Analyze the rendering workload of a scene and identify what is responsible for individual draw calls.

Scope

This should be broader than the Canvas Batch Analyzer.

Analyze:

* SpriteRenderers
* MeshRenderers
* SkinnedMeshRenderers
* ParticleSystems
* UI
* Materials
* Shaders
* Textures
* Cameras
* Rendering layers
* Batching
* Instancing


Additional analysis

Identify potential causes of additional draws:

* Different materials
* Different textures
* Different shaders
* Material instances
* Batching failures
* Particle materials
* Renderer settings

Important

This tool should measure and explain, not attempt to automatically optimize the scene.

Deliverable

One standalone SceneDrawCallAnalyzer EditorWindow.
